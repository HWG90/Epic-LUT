# EPIC LUT R5.7.3 :goose:
**STYLISH FREEDOM — Defend Freedom in Style.**

**SHARED LUT — YOUR LOOK, VISIBLE TO YOUR SQUAD**
Share exact Armor, Helmet and Pattern colors/materials/camo/emissives with compatible Epic LUT squadmates. Apply an Armory preset to share it. Both players need Epic LUT with **Configuration > Share full LUT appearance** enabled (On by default). Cape materials are excluded; oversized looks stay local. **Shared Lobby LUT remains in testing.**

**CUSTOM MODEL HOTFIX + MAINTENANCE**
- Read the effective custom LUT from numeric patch layers, instead of reporting a false conflict between patch_0 and patch_2.
- Basic Apply Preset matches Armory assignments, including valid material-only targets.
- Preview gore filtering and large-font Apply clipping fixed.
- Three Pattern quick swatches: **Color / Mat/Opac / Roughness**. Roughness uses Alpha; other channels stay intact.
- Right-click a LUT ID/name to copy its real ID in your configured format.

**MORE COLOR. LESS CLUTTER.**
Checked-region camo and custom tints; **Map LUTs to Gear / Auto-map** in Basic and Advanced; scrolling Armory previews; checkbox Save/Apply + Select All; binding-aware patch exports. Values commit on click-away, Delete resets with Undo, Tab completes preset names.
