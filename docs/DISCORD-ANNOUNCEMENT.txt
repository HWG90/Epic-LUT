# EPIC LUT R5.7.1 :goose:
**STYLISH FREEDOM — Defend Freedom in Style.**

**SHARED LUT — YOUR LOOK, VISIBLE TO YOUR SQUAD**
Share applied **Armor, Helmet and Pattern** edits with compatible squadmates: exact color, alpha, material, camo and emissive values. Apply an Armory preset to share it. Improved lossless compression fits larger looks; oversized looks stay local.

Both players need R5.7.1. **Configuration > Share full LUT appearance with Epic LUT users** is On by default and controls sending/receiving. Updates wait for settled edits and matching sender kits. Cape materials are excluded. **Shared Lobby LUT remains in testing.**

**MORE COLOR. LESS CLUTTER.**
- **Camo boxes** start unchecked: patterns/colorways/tints affect checked regions. Custom tints switch to **Current / custom**.
- **Map LUTs to Gear** in Basic and Advanced; **Auto-map** follows numbered imports and cycles across checked Armor, Helmet and Cape columns.
- Two quick **Pattern** swatches initialize below the table; the full editor replaces it with channels, import/export and Undo/Redo.
- 8-region layout, scrolling Armory gear/Pattern previews, checkbox Save/Apply and **Select All**; docked Tools opens.
- Values commit on click-away; **Delete** resets cells with Undo. **Tab** completes preset names. Paydex row import and held Preview poses.
- Fixed export crashes/cache errors; patches retain verified shared-resource assignments. Tested exports work in-game.
