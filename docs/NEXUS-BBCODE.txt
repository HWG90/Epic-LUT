[b]Epic [color=#F4D44C]LUT[/color] [/b]lets you edit equipped armor and helmet colors live in Helldivers 2 through the Epic LUT menu, or compatible MCM when available. Pick a color, see it on your gear, and restore the originals when you want a fresh start.

LUT means [b]lookup table[/b]: the color and material data your gear uses. We're requisitioning a new paint job, not new equipment.

[b]Built on CowboyBingus's original LUT discovery and Match Your Colors foundation.[/b] Thank you, CowboyBingus, for finding the path and sharing the tools that made this possible.

[size=4][b][color=#F4D44C]WHAT'S IN THE FIELD KIT[/color][/b][/size][/size]

[list]
[*][size=6]Live RGB and float editing through MCM/LUT Menu, with separate armor and helmet controls.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Find supported color fields and numbered rows under Base Color / regions, grouped into collapsible palettes. Base Color starts open; expand Detail, Inner, Outer, Tint, or Camo when you need them. Click a current-color square to open its picker, with the original color beside it. Advanced / float grid keeps the researched semantic fields and raw channels available.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Reset Armor and Helmet together while keeping your custom work, restore either target independently, or reset custom rows for a clean slate.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Export and import data-only .dbflut presets.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Load supported DDS/EXR palettes and extract supported LUT data from ZIP/RAR mod archives with the automatically started bundled helper. Importing a file does not apply it until you choose Apply; saved enabled edits can return on later launches.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Open a native Windows file picker from the in-game editor. Quick Load is the opening page. The local browser editor is optional.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Use the advanced local editor for a pixel grid, copy/paste, undo/redo, quick save, row presets, and camo controls.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Access CowboyBingus's original Match Your Colors settings through their original shared controls.[/size][/*]
[/list]

[b]Game editor[/b][/size]

[list=1]

[*][size=6]Close the game for the initial installation. Extract the latest Epic LUT R3 alpha package.[/size][/*]

[size=6]
[b]Your paint crew comes in the box.[/b]
Python and the codecs are bundled. Epic LUT unpacks its private runtime in the background and starts the helper automatically for equipped-target discovery and file actions. You do not need to install Python or run setup scripts. RAR extraction still needs 7-Zip.

The game-launched helper exits when the game closes or crashes, including while a picker is pending. The optional browser editor can be started manually with start_editor.ps1 and has an independent lifetime. No browser is needed for the Windows picker.

[size=4][b][color=#F4D44C]RECOLOR YOUR LOADOUT[/color][/b][/size][/size]

[list=1]
[*][size=6]Equip the armor and helmet you want to edit.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Press F10 for Quick Load, or open Armor / Helmet → Base Color / regions for individual colors. Advanced / float grid is there when you want the raw details.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Choose a discovered LUT, then click any current-color square in its visible row list. No extra region selector is needed to see the colors. Rows are data regions; their visible surfaces vary by item.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Commit an RGB or supported float change. The edited row activates live. Opening or cancelling a color picker leaves the document unchanged.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Save a .dbflut preset if you want to keep or share the palette data.[/size][/*]
[/list]
[size=6]
For an external palette, use Quick Load or Files / hotload → Import LUT / mod archive. The helper starts automatically. Choose your file in the Windows picker, review the target, and use Apply. Cancel leaves your gear unchanged. A successful import switches the original Match Your Colors setting Off through its own controls. Canceled and invalid imports do not change that setting. The optional Advanced editor opens the local browser interface.

Quick Load remembers the imported filename and cached extracted palette. Seeing the old RAR name does not mean it is being extracted again or automatically Quick Applied every launch.

Armor and helmet are independent targets. An archive name or matching table dimensions does not prove that a palette belongs to the gear you selected. Review the target before applying it. Archive extraction reads supported palette data; it does not install the original mod patch or execute package scripts.

[b]A note on glow and materials:[/b] RGB-only edits leave other channels untouched, but base color can tint emitted light. Full-float imports and material edits can change more than color. Where available, turn on effect-preservation controls if you want to keep the originals for those fields; preservation is optional, not a guarantee of identical shader appearance. Unconfirmed fields are identified rather than assigned invented meanings.

TL;DR - GLOWY [b]ON[/b]? Preserve [b]OFF[/b]
GLOWY [b]OFF[/b]? Preserve [b]ON[/b]

[i]That glow shorthand is not a universal on/off switch: it depends on the imported data and material. Base Color can tint existing glow either way.[/i]

[size=4][b][color=#F4D44C]RESTORE / RESET / UNINSTALL[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Restore original armor or helmet colors[/b] stops that target's custom application and keeps the edited document available. [b]Reset Custom LUT Rows[/b] resets the stored values from preserved originals and clears overrides. Use the correct target's controls, or [b]Reset Armor and Helmet[/b] to restore both together without throwing away your custom work.

To uninstall, restore both targets, disable Epic LUT through your loader, then close the game. Remove its loader entry or installed armor_lut_editor folder through the method you used to install it. Back up any palettes you want to keep first. Your settings, files, presets, and runtime cache live under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Epic LUT; they are not migrated from or deleted in older loader folders. The game-launched helper shuts down automatically. A manually started browser helper is independent and should be closed separately. Leaving your loader and MCM installed is fine.

[size=4][b][color=#F4D44C]KNOWN LIMITATIONS[/color][/b][/size][/size]

[list]
[*][size=6]The live targets are equipped local armor and helmet pieces. Weapons, equipped capes, skin, and Armory previews are outside the current live editor.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]LUT layouts, row counts, and visible surface mappings vary. [/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Some shader meanings remain research mappings. I did my best. Emission is not a confirmed general RGB field, and color edits can alter the hue of existing glow.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6][b]DDS input supports bounded 2D uncompressed RGBA16F/RGBA32F LUTs, including MULTI-MIP files. Epic LUT reads the full-resolution color table and validates the mip chain; lower-resolution levels are not applied as separate palettes. Cube, volume, array, and unsupported/compressed formats remain unsupported. EXR supports bounded, non-deep RGBA data. Other image formats are not automatically interchangeable.[/b][/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Not every game-resource archive is supported. Encrypted, malformed, oversized, compressed/nonstandard, or unsupported packages can be rejected.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Sharing and visibility between players, comprehensive mission/equipment transitions, controller/focus scenarios, and long sessions have not been comprehensively validated. Although I do plan to investigate a way to 'broadcast' your LUT to other players using this mod.[/size]

[/*]
[*][size=6]Edited native textures are retained until exit. Reaching the budget restores originals and pauses application; unlimited editing sessions are not promised.[/size][/*]
[*][size=6]Native compatibility depends on the game build (for now). [/size][/*]
[/list]
