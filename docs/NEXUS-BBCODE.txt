A massive thank you to [b]Scarpheon[/b] for the time, patience and care he has poured into helping me build a better tool for people. His testing, bug reports and continued assistance were vital to getting [b]Export to Patch[/b] implemented correctly. He kept trying builds, checking results in-game and helping me work through the problems until we got it right.

He also painstakingly [b]mapped all 26 bump maps by their actual column-2 R values[/b]. That turned a list of anonymous numbers into choices people can understand: dry and wet grime, suede, denim, corduroy, wool, leather, metal and more. The order matters, and he did the work to get it right. Every time someone picks a material by name instead of guessing, they benefit from his effort.

This deserves much more than a name at the bottom of a page. Scarpheon helped make Epic LUT easier to use and gave us the detail needed to make these features work properly. [b]Thank you, Scarpheon. Your work and continued help have made a huge difference.[/b]
