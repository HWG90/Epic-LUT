[size=4][b][color=#ff0000]S[/color][color=#ff7700]H[/color][color=#ffff00]A[/color][color=#00ff00]R[/color][color=#0000ff]E[/color][color=#9900ff]D[/color] [color=#ff00ff]L[/color][color=#00ffff]U[/color][color=#1e84cc]T[/color] - YOUR LOOK, VISIBLE TO YOUR SQUAD[/b][/size]
Share your currently applied [b]Armor, Helmet and associated Pattern[/b] edits with compatible Epic LUT squadmates, automatically. Apply a saved Armory preset to share that look. Complete supported tables keep exact RGBA, material, camo and emissive channels, rather than reducing your appearance to a few color swatches.

Both players need [b]R5.7.3[/b] and [b]Configuration > Share full LUT appearance with Epic LUT users[/b] enabled. The current default is [b]On[/b]; that one setting controls [b]both sending and receiving[/b]. Turn it Off to remove your advertised look and restore received appearances still owned by Epic LUT.

Updates wait for a stable squad lobby and settled edits. A received look applies only while the sender's visible body, Armor and Helmet kits match; your own outfit can be different. Improved lossless compression and deduplication support larger appearances, including up to 4,096 bindings within the lobby limit. Oversized looks stay local with an explanatory status while receiving remains available.

[b][u][color=#00ffff]Cape material LUTs[/color][/u][/b][b] are [i]excluded[/i] from lobby sharing in this build.[/b] Unmodified games and Match Your Colors-only clients do not interpret Epic LUT appearances. [b]Shared Lobby LUT remains in testing.[/b]
