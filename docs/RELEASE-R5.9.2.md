# Epic LUT R5.9.2

- Export selected LUTs as one combined patch or separate patches.
- Exported LUT names now use your custom names.
- Matching Pattern LUTs are included when enabled.

Open LUT Editor > Export and choose **Choose LUT Patches** to select what to export.

Includes Shared LUT multiplayer appearances, Armor/Helmet/Cape editing, Pattern colors and roughness, Armory presets, custom-model LUT support and the R5.9.0 performance and live slider fixes.

Keep your presets and settings. Enable one copy: BSL, LLL R30+ or API-2 MDL. MDL is optional on Nexus; LLL is GitHub only. Shared Lobby LUT remains in testing. Weapon editing stays in the separate experimental R6 preview, with no support offered.
